## Key takeaways

* It drafts. You decide what is kept.
* Choose the surface: ask to understand, edit to change, agent to span files.
* A prompt without acceptance tests is a wish.
* The evidence is the diff and the actual test output, not the summary.

## The exit ticket

Each participant records two things on the prepared form:

1. One bounded task they will attempt tomorrow.
2. How they will verify the result.

A task submitted with no verification step goes back, because the verification is the objective. Collect the tickets on the prepared form so that responses are retained, rather than on a shared screen where other people's answers are visible. Paper index cards collected at the door are an adequate fallback.

### Acceptance

* Mechanical gate: the form records one bounded task and one verification step.
* Judgment gate: the facilitator judges that the task is achievable in a single day.

## Questions

Questions cluster on entitlement, on using Copilot with private code, and on review duty. Answer the first two by pointing at the [resources page]({{ '/resources.html' | relative_url }}) rather than by speculating.

If a question asks about using Copilot with customer or regulated material, say that it is out of scope for this session and that it is a data-handling decision with its own owner. Do not improvise a policy answer.
